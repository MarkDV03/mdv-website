Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

This page explains what information Christina's Hair Salon collects through this website and what we do with it. The short version: we only use what you send us to get back to you, and we never sell it.

What we collect

Information you send us. When you use the appointment request form, we collect your name and phone number, and anything else you choose to add, such as the service you want, a preferred day and time, and notes for us.

Information collected automatically. This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own. Like any website, the company that hosts it (GitHub Pages) may keep basic technical logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and running.

How we use it

  • To call or text you back about your appointment.
  • To get your visit ready, such as the service and timing you asked for.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Other services on this site

Some parts of this page are provided by other companies. When your browser loads them, those companies may receive your IP address and set their own cookies, under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps, which shows the map to the salon.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts on this page.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and requests only as long as we need them to help you and to keep normal business records. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we honor them for everyone. We will not treat you differently for asking.

To make a request, call us at (408) 550-4392 or ask us at the salon.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
