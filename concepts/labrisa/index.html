Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still wondering? Call (510) 555-0150. A real person picks up.

How do I order pickup?

Call us with your order and we will have it bagged and ready in about 15 minutes. Online ordering is coming soon. Taco Tuesday pre-orders are welcome.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes. Rajas con queso, grilled veggie burritos and nopales tacos. Our beans are cooked without lard and our corn tortillas are naturally gluten-free.

How much notice do you need for catering?

48 hours for most orders, and about a week for events over 100 guests. We deliver to Hayward, San Leandro, Castro Valley and nearby.

Is there parking?

Free customer parking is right behind the building, with street parking on Example Ave. We are also a short walk from the bus line.
