FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Something else on your mind? Call (510) 555-0140 and a real person will pick up.

Do you charge a trip fee?

There is a flat $49 diagnostic visit, and it is waived when you approve the repair. You will always know the price before we start.

How fast can you get here in an emergency?

For burst pipes, gas smells near the water heater or sewage backups, we aim to arrive within 60 minutes anywhere in our service area, any hour.

Are your plumbers licensed and background-checked?

Yes. Every technician is a direct employee, background-checked, drug-tested and covered by our license and liability insurance.

Do you offer financing for bigger jobs?

Repipes, sewer replacements and water heaters can be split into monthly payments, subject to approval. Ask your plumber for options with your quote.
